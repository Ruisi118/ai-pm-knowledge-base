## How to Use These Templates

### For Quick Analysis
Use Template 1 (Competitive Product Research Canvas) with just the core dimensions filled out.

### For Deep Analysis
Complete all templates in sequence:
1. Start with Template 3 (Reverse Engineering) to understand the product deeply
2. Fill out Template 1 (Product Research Canvas) comprehensively
3. Use Template 2 (Competency Growth) to track your learning

### For Team Collaboration
- Assign different dimensions to different team members
- Compile findings in a shared document
- Hold review sessions to discuss insights

### For Long-term Intelligence Building
- Maintain a library of completed templates
- Track changes over time (e.g., product updates)
- Build a database of competitive insights
